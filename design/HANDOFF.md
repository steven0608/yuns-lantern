### Responsive rules (all engines)

1. **Touch targets never shrink.** 88 pt min, 24 pt slop, 64 pt gap on every device. Fit the layout to the targets, not the other way round (CLAUDE.md).
2. **Layout by the short side** (`MediaQuery.size.shortestSide`):
   - `< 500` (iPhone landscape, phone web): at most **2 rows** of targets; arrange up to 6 items as 3 × 2 or one row of up to 5. Items render at 88–104 pt.
   - `≥ 500` (iPad, desktop web): up to 3 rows; items render at 120–140 pt.
3. **No top bar.** Home (top-left) and hear-it-again (top-right) float in the corners inside the safe area, so the full height stays usable on iPhone.
4. **Safe areas.** Respect the notch / Dynamic Island on the left and right in landscape, and the home indicator at the bottom.
5. **Scene art is cover-cropped.** Every 1194 × 834 scene keeps its important content in the **central 1194 × 560 band**, so an iPhone crop (2.16:1) loses only sky and ground.
6. **Web desktop:** cap the stage at 1366 × 1024 and centre it on `paper` (#FBF1E1). Never stretch past it.
7. **Text for parents** uses Noto Sans SC (bundled subset); scale with the system text size in the parent area.

## 2. Phases (paste one prompt at a time)

### Phase V2: ship v2 to the web and TestFlight (in progress in the app session)
> Finish the 12 v2 engines per `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`. Make the Pages workflow deploy from the branch we actually use (`master`), with `--base-href "/<repo>/"`. Add the web start screen (tap Yun to begin, which also unlocks audio) and the portrait "turn your phone" picture. Apply the responsive rules in `design/HANDOFF.md` §1 to every engine, and add golden tests at 844 × 390 and 1194 × 834 in both locales. Accept when: all 12 games play on iPhone landscape, iPad and phone web with no target under 88 pt, and the Pages site loads offline after the first visit.

### Phase E1: v3 framework
> Read `docs/EXPANSION.md` §3–§5. Add an engine registry that reads `content/catalog.json` (engines, games, minAge, free) and `content/library.json` (themes, tales). Replace the home grid with 3 doors: Story (map), Play (Lantern Lands: one land per engine, 5 games each, never more than 6 targets on screen), Books (theme shelves → book reader). The book reader has "read to me", tap-to-turn, no auto-advance, and language EN, 中文 or Both (read in the main language, then the other). Locked content is invisible to the child. Accept when: the Numbers land and tales 1–5 work end to end in all three language modes, driven only by JSON; screens match the canvas boards "Home v3", "Lands", "Books" and "Reader".

### Phase E2: engines built from existing parts
> Implement engines `order_line`, `pattern`, `jigsaw`, `position`, `mirror`, `listen_find`, `memory_pairs` (shared DropTarget, TouchTarget and GentleHint) and generate rounds for their games in `tools/build_content.py`. Add a per-engine rule to `validate_content.py`: at most 6 interactive items. Accept when: every game in those engines has ≥ 10 rounds, validators pass, and there are golden tests per engine in both locales and both size classes.

### Phase E3: engines with new interactions
> Implement `try_see` (any guess leads to watching the result; no wrong answer), `trace` (drag along a path with generous tolerance; the dot leads, a hand demonstrates on idle), `color_fill` (open-ended, no score), `music_echo` (any tap sequence is praised; the pattern replays slower as the hint; never use the microphone). Accept when: the touch-target test, the no-fail-state test and the no-microphone audit pass for each engine.

### Phase E4: all 75 tales
> For each tale in `content/library.json` with `status: written`, wire page art from `assets/images/tales/<tale_id>/p<N>.png` and narration from `assets/audio/vo/{en,zh}/tales/<tale_id>/p<N>.mp3`. Tap a character to hear its word. The last page offers the linked game. Accept when: every written tale reads in EN, 中文 and Both, and the VO parity test covers `tales/`.

### Phase E5: launch material
> Build the landing page from `landing/` (produced by the design session), deploy it as the Pages site root, and move the web app to `/play/`. Accept when: the landing page makes no third-party requests (check in CI), works on a phone, and links to the App Store listing and to `/play/`.

### Phase R: App Store release
> StoreKit 2 unlock + restore, privacy policy page (from the landing page), App Privacy "Data Not Collected", Kids Category ages 5 and under, localized listings (English, 简体中文), screenshots at 6.9" iPhone and 13" iPad from the canvas screens, app preview video from real footage. Native-speaker Mandarin VO replaces TTS placeholders before submission; the release build fails if `assets/audio/vo/_PLACEHOLDER` exists.

## 3. Asset contract

- File names in `design/png/` are stable; new sets arrive in new folders. The app session re-runs `tools/sync_design_assets.py` when told.
- Engine (land) icons: `design/png/lands/<engine_id>.png`. Game icons: `design/png/games/<game_id>.png`. Vocab items: `design/png/items/<vocab_id>.png`. Tale pages: `design/png/tales/<tale_id>/p<N>.png`. Brand: `design/png/brand/`.
- Everything is exported at 2× the on-screen size, with a transparent background (except the app icon and scenes).

## 4. Definition of done, per game

- [ ] Reads every round from content; no round, word or number in Dart
- [ ] ≤ 6 interactive items; targets ≥ 88 pt at 844 × 390
- [ ] Miss 1 soft return, miss 2 pulse, miss 3 hand; idle 8 s replays the prompt, 20 s demonstrates
- [ ] Every touch gives sound + motion; celebration then replay-or-home, never auto-advance
- [ ] Plays with the screen reader off and no reading; all instructions are audio
- [ ] Golden tests: EN + 中文 × iPhone + iPad
- [ ] Web: works after first load with the network off
